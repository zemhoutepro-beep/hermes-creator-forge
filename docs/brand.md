# Brand notes

**Idea:** a rough workflow becomes a checked, reusable skill sheet. The left card represents source work; the arrow marks the transformation; the right sheet and check show the reviewed output.

**Geometry:** two simple page shapes, a single direction arrow, and one large verification mark. The same geometry appears in the light and dark logo; the compact icon keeps the output sheet and check at small sizes.

**Palette:** warm paper (`#F7F1E7`), ink slate (`#263541` / `#19242D`), and forge amber (`#B96B27` / `#EAA55D`). Amber identifies the transformation and verified result.

**Assets:** `assets/logo-light.svg`, `assets/logo-dark.svg`, `assets/icon.svg`. All are self-contained square SVGs. The primary mark describes workflow-to-skill creation instead of generic AI imagery.
